Resume an app on another computer from its hosting provider. `npx ownmail` now offers "Resume an app deployed from another computer", lists the OwnMail apps on your Cloudflare, Vercel, or Netlify account, and matches the one you pick to its Nylas app by its OwnMail API key. This replaces looking projects up by a Nylas app name tag, which sandbox apps cannot carry, so projects that were never found before can now be resumed.
