Three new calendar controls. You can zoom the day and week grid in four steps from the control at the right of the day header, and the grid keeps your place as it resizes. The time-zone label at the top of the time column now opens a small picker to add, change, or remove a second time zone without leaving the calendar. On desktop, a button at the start of the top bar hides the calendar sidebar to give the grid more room. Each choice is remembered on the device.
