Compose is no longer a page. One composer opens over whatever is on screen, from any module, and a reply is written inline under the last message of its thread. Opening another composer saves and closes the current one, sending confirms with a toast instead of leaving for Sent, and focus returns to whatever opened it. Old `/mail/compose` links still open the composer.
