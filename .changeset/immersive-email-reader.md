Read email the way it was designed, in dark mode too. Light-only messages are now adapted color by color instead of inverted: the sender's canvas blends into the dark reader, cards lift onto a surface, brand colors and dark sections keep their colors, and text stays readable. Messages whose logos sit on a white matte stay on a light sheet that runs edge to edge. Threads drop the boxed cards for one aligned column with the sender's canvas extending across the reading pane. Tables keep their rows on narrow screens, original layouts never shrink text below 80% (they scroll sideways instead), button links are no longer underlined, reopened messages keep their height, and "Always use original colors" can be remembered per sender from the thread display menu.
