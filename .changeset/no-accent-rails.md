Selected and highlighted items no longer use a coloured bar along one edge. Calendar invitation cards drop the heavy left stripe, the open conversation and current folder are marked with a fill, the keyboard cursor draws a full outline around its row, search errors show an alert icon inside a tinted box, quoted text in the composer is indented and muted, and the active mobile tab sits on a filled pill. Each state is also announced to assistive technology rather than conveyed by colour alone.
